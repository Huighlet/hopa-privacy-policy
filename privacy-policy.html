Privacy Policy for HOPA

Last Updated: November 2024

Introduction

HOPA ("we," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in our school reports management application.

Data Collection and Storage

What Data We Store

HOPA is a completely offline application. All data is stored locally on your device:

  • Student Information: Names, student numbers, classes, academic records
  • Academic Data: Grades, scores, progress reports, attendance records
  • User Account Information: Email addresses, PIN codes (encrypted), security questions
  • School Configuration: Classes, subjects, grading scales, academic years
  • Report Data: Generated report cards, master sheets, and related documents

How Data is Stored

  • All data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device
  • User authentication credentials are encrypted using secure storage
  • Database exports can be encrypted using AES-256-CBC encryption
  • No data is transmitted to external servers or third parties

Data Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone.

  • HOPA operates entirely offline
  • No data is sent to external servers
  • No analytics or tracking services are used
  • No third-party data sharing occurs

Data Export and Backup

  • You can export your data as JSON or SQL files
  • Exported files can be encrypted for security
  • Exports are stored on your device or shared at your discretion
  • You are responsible for the security of exported files

Data Security

  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • User PINs are encrypted using secure storage
  • Database exports can be encrypted with AES-256-CBC
  • School identity validation prevents cross-school data imports
  • Checksum validation detects tampering of exported data

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Access: You can access all your data through the app
  • Export: You can export your data at any time
  • Deletion: You can delete data through the app's data retention features
  • Control: You have complete control over your data

Children's Privacy

HOPA is designed for use by teachers and school administrators. Student data is managed by authorized school personnel only. We do not knowingly collect data directly from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact your school administrator or the app developer.

Developer Contact: huighlet.zowonu@htu.edu.gh

Compliance

This app complies with:

  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) principles
  • Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) guidelines
  • Local data protection regulations

Note: This is an offline application. All data remains on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.
